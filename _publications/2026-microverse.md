MicroVerse measures whether persona-conditioned agents maintain identity fidelity under sustained pressure. Agents carry an immutable "soul file" (core values, moral boundaries, personality, goals) and inhabit a resource-scarce environment where water is a non-respawning survival constraint, with an eight-verb action space that maps directly to moral boundaries.

## Measurement Design
- Identity drift is scored offline using a paraphrase-aware, value-anchored, multi-register diff rather than raw cosine similarity.
- Uniform longitudinal engine snapshots and a forced-end snapshot of all living and dead agents mitigate survivor bias.

## Findings
- Anti-self-deception emerges unprompted as the single largest semantic category of identity modification (24% of added boundaries).
- The system is threshold-robust: lower reflection gates accelerate revision frequency but preserve drift direction.
