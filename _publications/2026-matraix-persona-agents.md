MatrAIx is a population-scale simulated-user evaluation infrastructure for testing AI systems and digital products with heterogeneous users instead of costly human panels.

## Components
- **Persona 8B**: 8.3 billion persona records across 1,290 categorical dimensions, with a released quality-filtered coreset of ~1 million personas (599,847 human-grounded, 400,000 synthetic).
- **Playground**: four environments (Survey, AI Chatbot, Web, App) where diverse persona agents evaluate and interact with digital products.
- **Tasks**: 1,010 application tasks spanning more than 25 domains including Commerce, Software, Finance, and Healthcare.

## Validation
- In a 400-trial controlled study, declared behavior was expressed or correctly suppressed in 91.5% of trials across ten behavioral attributes and all four environments.
- 18,189 evaluation trials were run with persona agents powered by Claude Opus 4.8, GPT 5.5, and Claude Haiku 4.5.
