ASI-Bench evaluates frontier AI systems on research-grade scientific work at a scale beyond existing expert benchmarks. Its 60 project-level tasks span 11 scientific domains, with B1-B4 guidance-withdrawal control to measure how far agents progress as scaffolding is removed.
