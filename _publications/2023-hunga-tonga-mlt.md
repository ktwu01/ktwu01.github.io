Poster presented at AGU Fall Meeting 2023 (San Francisco, CA), investigating how the January 2022 Hunga-Tonga volcanic eruption perturbed the mesosphere and lower thermosphere (MLT) region.

## Research Focus
- Tracks wave perturbations from the eruption across four meteor radar stations by projecting winds toward the great-circle direction from the epicenter
- Compares meteor-radar observations directly against WACCM-X simulations in profile, amplitude, and wavelet space
- Re-confirmed that a single volcano can send shockwaves around the Earth, seen in both simulations and observations
