ResearchClawBench evaluates AI systems on end-to-end autonomous scientific research rather than isolated research subtasks. Its 40 tasks span 10 scientific domains, with each task grounded in a real published paper and evaluated using expert-curated multimodal rubrics.

## Evaluation
- Seven autonomous-research agents are evaluated under a unified protocol.
- Seventeen native large language models are evaluated through ResearchHarness.
- Error analysis focuses on experimental-protocol mismatch, evidence mismatch, and missing scientific core.
