CXBench scores both outcomes and procedures for tool-using customer-service agents across six regulated domains. Rule cards connect obligations to observable trace evidence, including disclosures, consent, and required steps. Reaching the desired business state alone cannot earn full credit. [CXBench: A Benchmark for Process-Compliant LLM Agents in Regulated Customer-Experience Workflows](https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2610.25954).

VibeTraining-Bench evaluates research agents that improve an entire agent system under a fixed resource budget. They can modify training data, the trainer, and the inference harness using validation feedback before selecting a final system. The test makes resource allocation across interacting components part of the task. [VibeTraining-Bench: Data-Trainer-Harness Co-Design for Iterative System Improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26342).

## Why this matters

Keep the constraints that govern real work inside the evaluation. A final outcome can hide a skipped obligation or an improvement bought with excessive resources.
