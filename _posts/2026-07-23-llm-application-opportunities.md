The most valuable application of LLMs may not be answering questions, but turning an expert job into a digital work unit that can be executed, reviewed, and audited.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

This research starts from the history of electricity and the personal computer to explain why the commercial opportunity of a new technology usually lies not in merely replacing old tools but in redesigning workflows. For LLMs, the opportunity is shifting from writing, summarization, and Q&A toward professional applications that connect to real systems, preserve evidence, execute continuously, and take on responsibility.
