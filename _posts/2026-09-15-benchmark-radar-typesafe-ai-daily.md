[Typesafe AI Daily](https://collected.ga/typesafe-ai-daily-benchmark-radar-a-living-database-and-search-engine-for-ai-benchmarks-and-eval/) (Strongly Typed AI News, September 12, 2026) made our paper the lead story of its wider newsroom scan, calling it the strongest signal across AI infrastructure, funding, research, and developer tools that day.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

The desk reads the paper as a direction the market is moving rather than an isolated announcement: capital, compute, and typed developer infrastructure starting to move together. It lists the arXiv cs.AI source alongside funding headlines (Crusoe, Fluidstack), OpenAI's Agents API, NVIDIA's Vera Rubin efficiency numbers, and GPT-6 Astra.

The same edition traveled further: a [LinkedIn share](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/chiefscientist_typesafe-ai-daily-benchmark-radar-a-living-share-7504531089483309056-9uO7/) and the outlet's own archive carry it to newsletter readers.

A lead-story slot in a typed-infrastructure newsletter reaches exactly the engineers who evaluate agents for a living. That audience overlap is hard to buy.
