[Strongly Typed AI News](https://collected.ga/typesafe-ai-daily-benchmark-radar-a-living-database-and-search-engine-for-ai-benchmarks-and-eval/), the AI news site Alexy Khrabrov runs at collected.ga, made our paper the lead story of its September 12, 2026 "Typesafe AI Daily" issue. Its crawler checked 49 sources that day and found 12 strong signals across AI infrastructure, funding, research, and developer tools, and it named Benchmark Radar the strongest of them.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

The desk reads the paper as a direction the market is moving rather than an isolated announcement: capital, compute, and typed developer infrastructure starting to move together. It lists the arXiv cs.AI source alongside funding headlines (Crusoe, Fluidstack), OpenAI's Agents API, NVIDIA's Vera Rubin efficiency numbers, and GPT-6 Astra.

Alexy then [shared the issue on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/chiefscientist_typesafe-ai-daily-benchmark-radar-a-living-share-7504531089483309056-9uO7/) from his own account, which carried it past the site's archive to his professional network.

A lead-story slot in a typed-infrastructure newsletter reaches exactly the engineers who evaluate agents for a living. That audience overlap is hard to buy.
