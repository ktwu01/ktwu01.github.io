[Moonlight](https://www.themoonlight.io/en/review/benchmark-radar-a-living-database-and-search-engine-for-ai-benchmarks-and-evaluation), the AI research-reading companion by Corca, published a literature-review page for our paper *Benchmark Radar: A Living Database and Search Engine for AI Benchmarks and Evaluation*.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

The review page gives readers the paper with AI assistance built in: text and image explanation, AI chat over the document, smart citations, translation, auto-highlighted key points, and scholar deep search for related papers.

Moonlight serves researchers who read papers with an AI colleague at their side. A review page there puts Benchmark Radar inside the reading workflow itself, next to the tools that explain, annotate, and connect it to related work.

See the [Moonlight review](https://www.themoonlight.io/en/review/benchmark-radar-a-living-database-and-search-engine-for-ai-benchmarks-and-evaluation), or browse all Benchmark Radar coverage on my [Media Coverage](/media/#benchmark-radar-moonlight-review) page.
