> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

That goal is close to mine. I write a plain-English note for most of my own work on this blog, and a page on a site built for plain-language summaries brings my papers to readers who would never open arXiv. The author page gathers them in one place.
