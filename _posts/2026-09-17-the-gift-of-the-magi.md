*O. Henry Museum, Austin*

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

“I miss you”
may not mean
I miss one person.

Perhaps, in that instant,
memory returned to me
the self I once was,
happy and brave.

Sometimes
I think of beautiful moments
and weep.

Sometimes I remember
that I am not the only one
fighting in this world.

There are so many good stories
still waiting to unfold.

Our lives and stories,
our research and our striving,
must go on.

Why should we grieve
the passing of time?

The boundless ideals of one person
can never be fully realized
within a finite body.

So why should we grieve?
