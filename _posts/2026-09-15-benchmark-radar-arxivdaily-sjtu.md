[arXivDaily](https://www.arxivdaily.com/universities/sjtu), the Chinese arXiv digest with per-university pages, lists our paper on its Shanghai Jiao Tong University page, following the SJTU affiliation on the author list.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

arXivDaily tracks thousands of papers per university with Chinese-language AI summaries, weekday updates, and subscription filters. A university-page listing puts the paper in front of SJTU readers browsing their own institution's output, in Chinese, on the day it appears.

University digests are how many Chinese researchers actually keep up with arXiv. Meeting them there matters as much as the arXiv listing itself.

See the [arXivDaily SJTU page](https://www.arxivdaily.com/universities/sjtu), or browse all Benchmark Radar coverage on my [Media Coverage](/media/#benchmark-radar-arxivdaily-sjtu) page.
