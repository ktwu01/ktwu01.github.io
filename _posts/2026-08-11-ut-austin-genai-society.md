How can a generative-AI student society do more than host talks, repost news, and chase the latest model?

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

Founded in 2024, the GenAI Society at UT Austin positions itself at the intersection of research exploration, industry entrepreneurship, and social-cultural discussion. This horizontal-vertical analysis places it back in UT Austin's AI development trajectory, sets it alongside AI student organizations on and off campus, and presses a more specific question: how does it turn short-term attention into sustained member participation, real on-campus use, and publicly reusable outcomes?

The report proposes an executable direction: organize cross-disciplinary students in 4-to-6-week cycles around real, low-risk campus problems to produce prototypes, risk cards, and retrospectives. The success metric isn't signup numbers; it's whether the outcomes are actually used, can be reproduced, and whether members are willing to keep participating.
