[alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.11115) hosts a discussion page for our paper *Benchmark Radar: A Living Database and Search Engine for AI Benchmarks and Evaluation*, with the abstract, author list, BibTeX citation, and open discussion.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

The page lists all authors with their organizations (Earth-Space-AI, Tacite AI, Hangzhou Dianzi University, CMU, Xi'an Jiaotong University, Auckland, SJTU, Tsinghua), the submitted PDF, a one-click BibTeX copy, and related papers such as the benchmark-saturation study our analysis builds on.

alphaXiv is where researchers read papers in the open and argue about them in comments. A page there means the paper can collect public questions, corrections, and follow-ups beyond the PDF.

See the [alphaXiv page](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.11115), or browse all Benchmark Radar coverage on my [Media Coverage](/media/#benchmark-radar-alphaxiv) page.
