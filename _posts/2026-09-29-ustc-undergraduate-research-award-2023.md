USTC recognized my project with the [2023 Undergraduate Research Plan Outstanding Award](https://www.teach.ustc.edu.cn/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/202333%E5%8F%B7-%E5%85%B3%E4%BA%8E%E6%8E%88%E4%BA%88%E8%94%A1%E6%AD%A3%E6%B5%A9%E7%AD%8919%E5%90%8D%E5%90%8C%E5%AD%A6%E5%A4%A7%E5%AD%A6%E7%94%9F%E7%A0%94%E7%A9%B6%E8%AE%A1%E5%88%92%E6%A0%A1%E7%BA%A7%E4%BC%98%E7%A7%80%E5%A5%96%E5%86%B3%E5%AE%9A%E7%9A%84%E9%80%9A%E7%9F%A5.pdf). The project used WACCM-X and observations to study how the 2022 Tonga eruption disturbed the mesosphere and lower thermosphere.
