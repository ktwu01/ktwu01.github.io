Contribute just one research skill and you can become a coauthor of a Nature paper.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

We are collecting exclusive skills from researchers worldwide to build the next generation of AI research tools.

Follow the instructions below and you'll have the chance to become a Nature coauthor. The more Skills you submit, the higher your ranking in the author list.

Extracted skills take effect locally first. Even if you don't submit to the public repo, you can still use them yourself.

I've run through it, the whole install-and-use process takes about 5 minutes.

Run `npm install -g @scienceintelligence/researchskills-extract` in your terminal to install this skill.

Then type `/researchskills-extract` in your Claude Code. If you're a Codex user, type `$researchskills-extract`.

If you prefer the web versions of ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude, you can also visit researchskills.ai/submit-manually to submit.

Come contribute your exclusive experience, and help us together advance the intelligence of scientific research.
