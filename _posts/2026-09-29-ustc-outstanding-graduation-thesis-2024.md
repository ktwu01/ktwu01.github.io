USTC awarded my undergraduate thesis a [2024 Outstanding Graduation Thesis Award](https://www.teach.ustc.edu.cn/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/%E6%95%99%E5%AD%97202418%E5%8F%B7-%E5%85%B3%E4%BA%8E%E6%8E%88%E4%BA%88%E5%BC%A0%E9%AA%90%E7%AD%8987%E5%90%8D%E6%9C%AC%E7%A7%91%E6%AF%95%E4%B8%9A%E7%94%9F2024%E5%B9%B4%E5%BA%A6%E6%A0%A1%E7%BA%A7%E4%BC%98%E7%A7%80%E6%AF%95%E4%B8%9A%E8%AE%BA%E6%96%87%E5%A5%96%E7%9A%84%E5%86%B3%E5%AE%9A.pdf). The thesis studied the 2022 Tonga volcanic eruption's disturbance of the mesosphere and lower thermosphere using WACCM-X simulations and observations.
