[Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lanceeliot/2026/08/26/using-eight-billion-ai-personas-for-psychology-research-has-its-ups-and-downs/) covered [MatrAIx](/publication/2026-matraix-persona-agents), a study I coauthored, as a new research instrument for psychology. The article explains how population-scale AI personas can make experiments faster and easier to repeat while stressing that simulated users are not substitutes for people.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

That balance is central to the work: AI personas can help researchers form hypotheses and explore variation, but their construction, biases, and model dependence still require careful validation.
