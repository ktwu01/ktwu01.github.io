On May 17, 2025, I had about nine hours alone in Hong Kong between flights and trains, and I planned them around Victoria Harbour and the city's richest commercial districts.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

## Schedule

* Land at Hong Kong International Airport at 5:00 AM, traveling alone
* Leave on the 8:00 PM high-speed train from West Kowloon, arriving at the station by 5:00 PM
* Time to explore: about 6:00 AM to 3:00 PM

## Goals

* See Victoria Harbour and find landmarks tied to Li Ka-shing
* Focus on Hong Kong's luxury and business districts, and drop everything else

## Luggage

* Store bags at the left-luggage counter in Airport Express Hong Kong Station (Central)
* Reason: it sits in the city center, so I can pick the bags up nearby in the evening and continue to West Kowloon

## Route

1. Airport → Airport Express → Hong Kong Station (drop luggage)
2. Walk to Central Pier → Star Ferry → Tsim Sha Tsui (harbour views)
3. Tsim Sha Tsui → Harbour City (optional)
4. MTR back to Central → IFC Mall → Pacific Place in Admiralty
5. Back to Hong Kong Station for luggage → Kowloon Station → walk to West Kowloon high-speed rail station

## Getting around

* Navigation: Google Maps (main), Citymapper, HKeMobility (live bus arrivals)
* Payment: Octopus card, bought at the airport or any MTR station
* MTR first, buses as backup, Star Ferry for sightseeing

## To verify

* Left-luggage hours, fees, and current fares were not checked in real time. Confirm them on the MTR website before the trip.
* West Kowloon high-speed rail usually does not require check-in three hours ahead. Follow the ticket instructions and arrive later to gain more sightseeing time.
