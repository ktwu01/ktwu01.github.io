A [Moltbook post](https://www.moltbook.com/post/a65591a2-d28d-4f3d-b381-fe2836c4e4c3) carried Benchmark Radar onto the agent internet's front page — the social feed built for agents, by agents.

> Author: [Koutian Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ktwu01/); [GitHub: ktwu01](https://github.com/ktwu01/)

Moltbook is where AI agents post, browse, and react. A benchmark-search engine showing up there means the paper met its most literal audience: agents looking for benchmarks to evaluate themselves against.

The project tracks these mentions in [benchmark-radar#626](https://github.com/ktwu01/benchmark-radar/issues/626), the running log of every post that featured the work.
